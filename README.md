# 🚀 ParakhIQ – AI Resume Intelligence Platform

ParakhIQ is an AI-powered Resume Intelligence Platform that analyzes resumes, predicts suitable career roles, identifies skill gaps, and generates actionable recommendations to improve career readiness.

Unlike traditional ATS-style systems that primarily focus on keyword matching, ParakhIQ combines machine learning, explainable analysis, and AI-powered recommendations to provide deeper insights into a candidate's profile.

## 📈 Evolution of ParakhIQ

| Version                      | Objective                                                       | Key Contributions                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **V1 – Resume Shortlisting** | Automate resume screening                                       | PDF parsing, text preprocessing, TF-IDF based Resume–JD matching, candidate ranking                                  |
| **V2 – Role Recommendation** | Move beyond ranking and identify suitable career paths          | Best-fit role recommendation, skill-based matching, explainable scoring, skill-gap analysis                          |
| **V3 – Resume Intelligence** | Transition from rule-based logic to data-driven decision making | Multi-dataset training, TF-IDF feature engineering, Logistic Regression, SMOTE balancing, ML-powered role prediction |
| **Production Platform**      | Deliver a complete end-to-end user experience                   | Django web application, Resume Intelligence Dashboard, AI-powered recommendations, responsive UI, cloud deployment   |

## 🎯 Why ParakhIQ?

Most resume analyzers stop at matching keywords or calculating similarity scores.

ParakhIQ focuses on resume intelligence by answering critical questions:

* Which career role best aligns with a candidate's profile?
* What skills are currently missing?
* How strong is the resume overall?
* What improvements can increase career readiness?
* Why was a particular recommendation generated?

The goal is not just evaluation, but actionable guidance.
